# Acoustic stress clips (optional)

This folder is for **real CC0 / CC-BY speech or noise** used only to stress ASR
(codec, babble, wind). Clips here are **not** scenario goldens — do not attach
`title_must_include` expectations to them.

## Policy

- Prefer **CC0** or **CC-BY** (not CC BY-NC).
- Do not vendor AlloSat, DasanCallDial, or other NC telephony corpora.
- Keep files short (<15 s) and document attribution below when added.
